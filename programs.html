FAQ

Answers to common questions about how to access and use Kynkode services.

How do I sign up for Kynkode?

Signing up is simple through our digital portal. Pet owners can create a secure account, while veterinarians and shelters can apply for professional access to our unification mainframe.

How does the digital pet ID work?

The ID creates a futuristic digital twin of your pet. When a microchip is scanned, it links directly to your encrypted profile, allowing verified professionals to contact you instantly.

What happens when a pet is found?

When a pet is scanned at a vet or shelter, the finder accesses our global registry. You receive an immediate alert via the Kynkode network with the pet's current location and identification status.

Is my personal data secure?

Absolutely. Kynkode uses advanced technocratic verification protocols to ensure owner data is only accessible to verified partner clinics and authorized rescue organizations during reunification.
